The Core Concept

The Old Way

Dense Model

Like a "Jack of all trades." Every time you ask a question, the entire brain activates to compute the answer. As the brain gets bigger, it gets much slower and more expensive to run.

The MoE Way

Mixture of Experts

Like a "Hospital Triage." A Router looks at your problem and sends it only to the relevant specialists. A massive brain, but only a tiny, fast fraction activates per word.

Why does MoE Matter?

Sparse Activation

A 47B parameter model might only use 13B active parameters per token, meaning it runs much faster.

Domain Mastery

Experts naturally specialize during training, becoming incredibly proficient at specific syntaxes or concepts.

Compute Efficiency

You can massively scale the total knowledge capacity of the model without exploding the inference compute cost.
